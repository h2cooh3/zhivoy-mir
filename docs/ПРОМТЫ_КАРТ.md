# Промты для карт мира «Южное побережье»

Для генерации картинок (английский лучше понимается). Подписи на картах нейросети искажают, поэтому в промтах «NO TEXT»: подписи накладываются потом (`tools/maplabels.py`, чистые карты в `presets/maps/clean/`). Всё, чего нет в каноне, здесь придумано и ждёт решения автора.

## Блок стиля (в начало каждого промта)
```
Hand-painted medieval fantasy close-up map in the style of Kingdom Come: Deliverance 1 and 2 in-game maps crossed with clean anime-style linework: aged parchment, warm sepia, thick confident ink outlines, soft watercolor shading, charming pictorial miniature buildings, trees and tiny people, muted earthy palette (sand, ochre, olive green, brick-red roofs, slate-grey sea). Slightly oblique bird's-eye view. Thin double decorative border with small corner flourishes, a small compass rose in a corner, an empty scroll banner at the bottom-left. Landscape 4:3. ABSOLUTELY NO TEXT, no letters, no numbers anywhere; leave label areas clean. Negative prompt: text, letters, watermark, photorealistic, 3d render, modern objects, blur.
```

## 1. Деревня Солтхэм (подробно)
```
Detailed map of the village of Saltham, medieval southern coast. A main sandy road runs from the forest in the north through the center to a small stone-and-wood pier in the south. Center: a market square with a stone well, a few stalls with awnings, a small shop with a striped awning beside it. West of the square: a small stone chapel with a slender bell tower and a fenced graveyard with crosses and tall dark cypresses. South near the shore: a larger two-storey tavern with a hanging anchor sign. About 25 cottages of light stone with red clay-tile roofs and a few poor thatched ones, small vegetable gardens, fences, hay carts, chickens and dogs. Long narrow strip fields to the north, vineyards to the east, an olive grove along the coast road. At the far west edge, apart from the village, a lone yard on a narrow stream with a carpenter's workshop with a big water wheel, a hayrick, and a cow paddock. A small fisherman's family house at the fishing bay with nets drying on poles. Warm morning light.
```

## 2. Двор Уилла (мастерская у ручья)
```
Close-up map of a lonely carpenter's yard on the west edge of a village, on the bank of a narrow clear stream. A one-storey stone-and-timber house with a tiled roof and a small porch; next to it a large workshop with a big wooden water wheel in the stream, a millrace, stacks of cut planks, logs, a sawing trestle, a lathe under a lean-to, finished chairs and a half-built boat hull on trestles. A hay loft barn, a fenced paddock with two cows and a brown horse, a vegetable patch, a small orchard, a well, a woodshed. A footbridge across the stream; a dirt path leading east toward the village and a path up to a neighbouring sheep farm. Evening golden light, smoke from the chimney, quiet and lonely mood.
```

## 3. Рыбацкая бухта (ночь шторма)
```
Close-up map of a small rocky fishing bay on a stormy night. A curved bay with a short shingle beach. On the shore a modest family cottage of a fisherman with a vegetable patch and an overturned boat beside it, a boat shed, nets drying on tall poles, barrels and crates. Several rowing boats pulled up on the sand, one boat torn loose and drifting in the surf. A path leads from the bay to the village road and a footpath west along the cliffs toward dark rocks. Heavy grey waves, white foam, wind-bent grass, dark rain clouds shadow, a faint lantern glow in the cottage window. Dramatic moody lighting.
```
